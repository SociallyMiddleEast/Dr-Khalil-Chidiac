Meet your doctor

Trained in Beirut and Paris, practising close to home.

Dr. Chidiac trained in general and internal medicine and in gastroenterology at the Lebanese University, then completed his gastroenterology residency at Université Paris VII and a university diploma (DIU) in pancreatology at Université Paris V. He has been in practice since 2017 and treats patients at hospitals across Metn and Beirut.

How I can help

Not sure what you need? Start with a consultation and we'll decide together whether any tests or procedures are needed.

Consultations

Heartburn, abdominal pain, bloating, changes in bowel habits, and second opinions.

Gastroscopy

Upper endoscopy to examine the oesophagus, stomach and duodenum.

Colonoscopy

Examination of the colon for symptoms, screening and polyp follow-up.

Intragastric balloon

Endoscopic, non-surgical weight-loss support with follow-up.

Band ligation

Endoscopic banding for varices and selected bleeding sources.

Liver & pancreas

Hepatitis, fatty liver, abnormal liver tests and pancreatic disorders.
